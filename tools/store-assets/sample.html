Pistachio gelato at home

A weekend recipe · 20 minutes of work, one night of waiting

Good gelato needs less air and more flavour than ice cream. Toast the pistachios lightly, steep them in warm milk, and let the base rest overnight so it churns smooth and dense.

Method

  1. Toast 150 g pistachios for 6 minutes, then grind them fine.
  2. Warm the milk and cream, stir in the pistachios, and steep for 30 minutes.
  3. Whisk the yolks with the sugar, temper them with the warm milk, and cook to 82 °C.
  4. Strain, chill overnight, then churn until it holds soft peaks.

Ingredients

  • 500 ml whole milk
  • 150 ml double cream
  • 150 g shelled pistachios
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 110 g sugar
