# Scoop privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Scoop collects no data. It has no servers, makes no network requests, and
contains no analytics or tracking.

## What Scoop does with page content

When you turn Scoop on in a tab and pick an element, Scoop reads that element
from the page and writes a copy of it to your clipboard. This happens only in
your browser, only in the tab you turned it on in, and only when you click or
press Enter. Scoop does not keep what it copies and sends it nowhere.

## What Scoop stores

Scoop remembers one setting, the copy mode you last used (Full HTML, Clean
HTML, Plain Text or Markdown). It is kept in your browser's extension storage
on your device and is never sent anywhere. Removing the extension deletes it.

## Permissions

- `activeTab` lets Scoop act only on the tab where you start it, and only after
  you click its toolbar icon or press its shortcut.
- `scripting` lets Scoop add its highlight box and label to that tab.
- `storage` lets Scoop remember the copy mode you last used.

## Changes
